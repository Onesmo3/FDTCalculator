About This App

FDT Calculator — Sand Cone Method is a field tool for QA/QC on road construction projects (AASHTO T191 / ASTM D1556), built to help geotechnical/civil field technicians calculate Field Density Test results quickly and accurately, directly on site — with no internet connection required. The app stores your settings (cylinders, layers, project info) and test results directly on your device.

Version: 1.0.0

Privacy Policy

  • No server: All data — settings, project info, logos, and test results (Test Log) — is stored ONLY on your device. Nothing is sent over the internet or to any server.
  • No tracking: This app has no analytics, ads, cookies, or any form of user tracking.
  • PDF/CSV: Reports are generated directly on your device (offline). They are never uploaded or passed through any server.
  • Device permissions: The app may request permission to save files (downloading PDF/CSV) and access photos (logo upload). These permissions are used solely for that purpose.
  • Sharing with third parties: No data is shared with any outside company or service.
  • Deleting your data: Use "Clear Log" inside the app to delete results, or clear the app's data/cache in your phone settings to remove everything stored.

Self-Test

Run a built-in check against a known real-world FDT report (Nyerere Road, G7 Layer, n=4) to confirm the calculator's formulas and Table 7205/1 spec values are working correctly. Useful after any settings changes or app updates.

Backup & Restore

Share a backup of all your settings and saved results (cylinders, layers, project info, Single Point log, and Worksheet log) to Google Drive, email, or any app on your device. Restoring a backup will replace everything currently saved on this device.
